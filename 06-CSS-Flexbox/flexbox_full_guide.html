Detailed Notes for GitHub:

  • display: flex; - Enables Flexbox on the parent container.
  • flex-direction: - Defines the "Main Axis". row is horizontal, column is vertical.
  • justify-content: - Controls items along the Main Axis. Use space-between to push items to the edges.
  • align-items: - Controls items along the Cross Axis. center is the magic property for perfectly centering items.
  • flex-wrap: - Prevents your items from getting squished. wrap is essential for mobile-friendly layouts.
  • flex-grow: - Applied to children. A value of 1 tells that item to expand and take up all available empty space.
